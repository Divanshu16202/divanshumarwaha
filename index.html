Corporate Financial Strategy · Benchmarking

Reliance Industries vs. PSU Oil & Gas Peers: Multi-Ratio Stress Test

Context: Examining how capital allocation, non-oil diversification (Jio, Retail), and debt gearing differentiated Reliance from state-run energy giants (IOCL, ONGC).
Methodology: Three-stage DuPont Decomposition (Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier), WACC estimates, working capital cycles, and coverage ratio stress-testing in Excel.
Key Insight: Revealed how consumer business revenues shielded Return on Equity (ROE) against refining margin cyclicality, while identifying working capital drag in peer PSU inventories.
Deliverable: Institutional-grade 5-year benchmarking deck, liquidity matrix, and DuPont sensitivity models.
